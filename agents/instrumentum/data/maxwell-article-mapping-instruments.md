## Citation Format

### Standard Citation

```
Maxwell, J.C. (1873). A Treatise on Electricity and Magnetism.
  Part IV, Chapter XVII, Art. 730-750: Galvanometry
  Part III, Chapter IX, Art. 449-474: Magnetic Measurements
```

### In-Text Citation

```
According to Maxwell (Art. 730-750), the galvanometer
sensitivity depends on the coil geometry and field strength.

Maxwell's discussion of magnetic measurements (Art. 449-474)
describes both deflection and vibration methods.
```

## Historical Context

Maxwell's Treatise (1873) describes instruments of the era:

- Mechanical galvanometers (no electronics)
- Magnetic needle instruments
- Electromechanical electrometers
- Null methods for precision
- Visual observation (no digital displays)

Modern instruments build on Maxwell's principles while using:
- Electronic amplification
- Digital readouts
- Computer control
- Modern materials
