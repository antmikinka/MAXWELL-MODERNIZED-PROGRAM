## Composite Materials

### Maxwell's Mixing Theory

| Article | Topic | Application |
|---------|-------|-------------|
| Art. 314 | Conducting sphere in dielectric | **Maxwell-Garnett formula source** |

**Maxwell-Garnett Formula:**
K_eff = K_m × [(K_i + 2K_m + 2f(K_i - K_m)) / (K_i + 2K_m - f(K_i - K_m))]

This formula appears in Maxwell's discussion of a sphere in a dielectric medium (Vol. I, Art. 314).

## Citation Format

### Standard Citation

```
Maxwell, J.C. (1873). A Treatise on Electricity and Magnetism.
  Part I, Chapter IV, Art. 50-62: Specific Inductive Capacity
  Part III, Chapter IX, Art. 424-440: Magnetic Induction
```

### In-Text Citation

```
According to Maxwell (Art. 60-62), the constitutive relation for
dielectrics is D = KE = E + 4πP (CGS).

Maxwell's discussion of magnetic induction (Art. 424-440) establishes
B = μH = H + 4πI.
```
